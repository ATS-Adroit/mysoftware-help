Selecting colors

To use a predefined color: The basic color palette provides 48 predefined colors.

  1. Click the browse button to the right of the color box.
  2. Click the required color from the Basic colors section of the color dialog.
  3. Click Ok to accept this choice.

 

To create a custom color: You can define and use up to 16 additional custom colors in a color refiner box by specifying the hue, saturation, luminosity, and relative amounts of red, green, and blue.

  1. Click the browse button to the right of the color box.
  2. Move the mouse pointer across the color palette and clicking at the required position

Tip: Alternatively specify the proportion of RED, GREEN and BLUE primary colors, together with the hue, saturation and luminescence, as numbers from 0 to 255 directly.

  • hue is the position of the color along the color spectrum.
  • saturation is the purity of the hue, moving from grey to the pure color.
  • luminosity is the brightness of the color on a scale from black to white.
  1. Click the Add to Custom colors button.
  2. Click this color from beneath the Custom colors heading.
  3. Click Ok to accept this choice.
