How Maximum Demand is Calculated

Maximum demand or peak kVA demand is an additional cost for most non-domestic power users. Your kVA usage is averaged over a “block” period, ONLY during defined periods of the day when electricity consumption is high.

Block periods are typically 30 minutes but could be anywhere from 15-60 minutes in duration. If you are in doubt about the block period that is applicable for you, please speak to your electricity provider for clarification.

The block period with the highest kVA value, over the duration of the current billing period (month), is your maximum electricity demand.

Maximum demand calculation example:

Assuming that the power consumption on a plant over a block period of 30 minutes is comprised of 5 different episodes of consumption as follows:

  • 800 kVA for 5 minutes
  • 200 kVA for 5 minutes
  • 400 kVA for 10 minutes
  • 800 kVA for 8 minutes
  • 1500 kVA for 2 minutes

Adroit calculates the maximum demand for that 30 minute period as follows:

((800*5) + (200*5) + (400*10) + (800*8) + (1500*2)) / 30 = 613.33 kVA

During each of the above five episodes the predicted maximum demand would have been:

  • Episode1: (800*30) / 30 = 800 kVA
  • Episode2: ((800*5) + (200*25)) / 30 = 300 kVA
  • Episode3: ((800*5) + (200*5) + (400*20)) / 30 = 433.33 kVA
  • Episode4: ((800*5) + (200*5) + (400*10) + (800*10)) / 30 = 566.66 kVA
  • Episode5: ((800*5) + (200*5) + (400*10) + (800*8) + (1500*2)) / 30 = 613.33 kVA

If this agent had a configured maximum demand limit of 600 kVA and the Enable max demand alarm checkbox is enabled, then the MaxDemand status bit would have been on during episodes 1 and 5.
