About Alarm Management

Alarm management is concerned with improving the QUALITY of the information within the alarming configuration. This information should be used to reduce the QUANTITY of nuisance alarms (red herrings) so that only the most important and relevant alarms are displayed for your process.

In other words, alarm management can counteract inefficient alarming. Up to now SCADA installations have used numerous alarms, each of which usually have very little awareness of the other alarms in the system. So generally each incident generates a flood of alarms, which operators simply ignore because there are too many to deal with.

According to the EEMUA 191 guidelinesa document created by the Engineering Equipment & Materials Users' Association ( EEMUA) of alarm management guidelines, developed by the users of alarm systems, and is the globally accepted and leading guide to good practice for alarm management.:

  • An alarm is an incident that an operator must understand, acknowledge and respond to – not simply acknowledge and ignore. For further details, see Effective Alarm Characteristics.
  • No process should have more than 6 such alarm occurrences an hour during normal operations.
  • Under abnormal process conditions no more than 10 alarms per minute should occur.

Your SCADA installation requires an alarming system that alerts the operator to the most relevant alarm for the current incident. In this case fewer alarms are more effective as the human mind can only focus on a few things at a time. Adroit can help you to achieve this alarming system as follows:

  1. The AlarmManagement agent can help you by generating statistics from the data produced by the alarming system. This allows you to have efficient and meaningful incident reviews to identify and remove nuisance alarms by analysing the counts, frequencies, durations and acknowledgement times of incidentssignified by AgentName.AlarmType, which indicate that this agent has gone into the condition or state specified by this alarm type. Each incident can either be an alarm (requiring action) or an event (not requiring action)..

Note: By repeating this process regularly, you will be able to ensure that only relevant alarms are generated thereby improving your alarming system.

  1. Adroit has improved the legacy alarming system, to allow you to assign a priority PER ALARM (previously priorities could only be assigned PER AGENT), which allows your operators to filter out lower priority alarms, if required.
