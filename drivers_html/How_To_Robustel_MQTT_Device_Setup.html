Router/Client Can't Connect with the Broker
Verify the Broker login credentials, that TLS is
enabled, that broker certificate is still valid
and applied to the client.
Check internet connectivity.
Verify if other devices or clients also
experience a connection problem, or do
other clients still connect with the broker
successfully.
Login to the broker and see that broker
subscription & service is still active and that
we have not reached max client connections
or that the device sim ran out of data.
Modbus Register/s not updating
Verify that Modbus Client is enabled.
Verify Modbus table, IP address, Port, TCP
selection, Modbus function and registers to
be configured correctly.
Check PLC connection and availability. Use
Router ping tools to poll the local devices
connected to its LAN0 port.
Verify that the PLC's Modbus routing table is
configured and setup correctly.
